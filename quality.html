Quality & compliance

Sourced with documents in hand.

We work with GMP manufacturing partners and prepare the paperwork that serious buyers expect. This website does not make therapeutic claims, and we do not present products as TGA-listed unless that status is confirmed for a specific item.

Source

Match the category to manufacturing partners who can describe their quality system and supply supporting statements.

Specify

Agree format, potency range, packing, and the document set before supply is arranged.

Document

Specifications, certificates of analysis, and related batch files are prepared for partner review.

Deliver

Export and import paperwork is organised as agreed, so handover is orderly rather than improvised.

Available on request

Typical partner files — confirmed per product and batch, not assumed from this page.

  • Spec sheets Composition, format, and packing outlines for the item under discussion.
  • Certificates of analysis Batch-level COA from the manufacturing partner, as available.
  • Manufacturing statements GMP or equivalent system statements from the producing site.
  • Import readiness Support for Australian complementary-medicine and import expectations, item by item.

What this site will not do

We will not say a product cures, treats, or is “clinically proven” here. We will not imply TGA listing without confirmation. Health products deserve a quieter register: files, specifications, and honest limits.

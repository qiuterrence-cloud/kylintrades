Why Kylin

A quiet standard of care.

A name of care

The kylin (qilin) has long been associated with good fortune and protection. We keep the symbolism light: care in what we source, and care in how we trade.

Australia, and a trade bridge

We work from Sydney, sourcing from Australian agriculture and native botanicals, and trading with partners in China and beyond. Organic and traceable supply where the files support it. Cross-border, locally accountable.

Built for the long term

Wholesale relationships, not catalogue clicks. Specifications, batches, and continuity matter more to us than a storefront.
